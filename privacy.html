Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 13, 2026

Canvas Resource Sidekick ("the Extension") is committed to your privacy.

1. Data Collection & Processing

  • Local Processing: The core functionality (detecting and downloading Canvas files) happens entirely on your local machine. No course data or files are ever transmitted to our servers.
  • Beta Waitlist (Optional): If you choose to join the beta waitlist via the "Notify Me" feature, your email is sent to Formspree solely for update notifications. This is voluntary and not required to use the extension.

2. Permissions Justification

  • scripting: Used to identify file download links within Canvas course modules.
  • downloads: Used to save your course resources directly to your computer.
  • storage: This permission is not used to adhere to the principle of least privilege and data minimization.

3. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please use the Support Form linked above.
