Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Canvas Downloader ("the Extension") is committed to your privacy.

1. Data Collection & Processing

  • Local Processing: The core functionality (detecting and downloading Canvas files) happens entirely on your local machine. No course data or files are ever transmitted to our servers.
  • Feedback Forms (Optional): If you submit feedback using the extension's popup or external links, the information you provide (such as your email and message) is sent securely for the sole purpose of providing support and improving the extension.

2. Permissions Justification

  • scripting: Retained for a legacy programmatic injection handler; the current downloader scripts are loaded through the manifest.
  • downloads: Used to save your course resources directly to your computer.
  • storage: Used to save settings in Chrome sync storage, which can sync between signed-in browsers, such as your preference for organizing files into a 'Canvas Resources' folder.
  • tabs: Used to inspect the active tab and send status and navigation messages to content scripts.

3. Website Access & Storage

The extension requests access to all HTTP and HTTPS websites to support custom Canvas domains and externally hosted resources. Its scripts load on matching pages and use Canvas page landmarks to decide when to scan and display controls. Course scan results are cached in the Canvas tab sessionStorage. Resource requests go to Canvas and file hosts using browser authentication where applicable. No analytics or developer upload endpoint is configured. The popup feedback endpoint is not configured; use the optional external Google Form for support. Information you submit there is processed by Google Forms.

4. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please use the Support Form linked above.
