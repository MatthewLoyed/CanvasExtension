Privacy Policy

Canvas Resource Sidekick: Bulk File Downloader values your privacy.

We collect no personal data through the use of this extension. Your browsing history, Canvas data, and credentials never leave your browser and are not transmitted to any external servers.

Emails provided for the beta waitlist are stored locally and privately within the extension's storage. These emails are collected solely to gauge interest and notify you of future updates. We do not sell or share this information with third parties.
