# Canvas LMS Bulk Downloader & Resource Saver (Chrome Web Store Listing)

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Master your Canvas materials. Effortlessly bulk download slides, PDFs & docs with smart organization. Your central study hub.

## Long Description

### ⚡ The Problem: Canvas Downloads are tedious.
Mastering your courses shouldn't mean spending hours clicking "Save Link As" on every single lecture slide.

### 🌟 The Solution: One-Click Bulk Download
**Canvas LMS Bulk Downloader** scans your current Canvas course page (Modules, Files, or Assignments) and identifies every available resource. 

With a single click, it downloads them all to your computer.

*   **⚡ Instant Detection**: Finds PDFs, PowerPoints, and Word Docs directly on the page.
*   **🛡️ Smart Resolution**: Automatically resolves tricky "preview" links to get the actual file, not an HTML wrapper.
*   **✅ Download Tracking**: Visual indicators show you exactly which files you've already grabbed.
*   **🔒 Private & Secure**: runs purely in your browser. No data collection.

### 🎓 Perfect for:
*   Backing up your course materials before the semester ends.
*   Downloading all readings for offline study.
*   Grabbing every assignment rubric and prompt in one go.

**Works with any Canvas LMS instance**, including Instructure, University self-hosted Canvas, and more.
