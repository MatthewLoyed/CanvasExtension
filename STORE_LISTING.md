# Canvas Downloader - Bulk Save Modules & Course Files

## Short Description (132 chars max)
Effortlessly bulk download Canvas course materials. One-click saver for modules and files.

## Long Description

### Canvas Downloader: The Ultimate Resource Saver
**⚡ Stop the "Scavenger Hunt" on Canvas**
Don't waste hours manually clicking "Save Link As" for every lecture slide. Canvas Downloader scans your current course page (Modules, Files, or Assignments) to find resources instantly. One click is all it takes to download all the files on your screen.

### 🌟 Key Productivity Features
*   **⚡ Instant Bulk Download**: Automatically detects PDFs, PowerPoints (.pptx), Word docs, and Excel sheets directly on your current page.
*   **🛡️ Smart Preview Resolution**: Our API-first strategy resolves tricky "HTML wrapper" links to grab the direct download URL for every file.
*   **✅ Visual File Menu**: A quick-access dropdown menu shows exactly which files are available on the page and displays download progress.
*   **🔒 Secure & Private**: No data ever leaves your browser. We use your existing Canvas session to keep your academic data 100% private.

### 🎓 Built for Every Student
Whether you’re a ChromeOS user managing school-issued hardware or a power user on Windows, this extension is designed to be lightweight and non-intrusive.

*   **Prep for Finals**: Grab all readings for offline study in seconds.
*   **Offline Access**: Perfect for studying during commutes or in areas with limited Wi-Fi.
*   **Zero-Config Setup**: Works instantly with MSU, Harvard, Sydney, and any university using a custom Canvas domain.
