## Long Description

### 🚀 Stop Clicking Files One by One.

Mastering your courses shouldn't mean spending hours manually downloading lecture slides and syllabus. **Canvas LMS Bulk Downloader** transforms your Canvas experience into a streamlined study workflow.

With a single click, our extension scans your current Canvas course page, identifies every resource, and saves them to your computer—neatly organized and ready for offline study.

### 🌟 Key Features

*   **⚡ One-Click Bulk Download**: Grab every file on a page instantly. Perfect for "Files" tabs, Modules, and Assignment pages.
*   **🌈 Modern Premium UI**: A sleek, dark-mode-ready interface designed with micro-interactions and smooth animations for a superior feel.
*   **📁 Automatic Organization**: Files are automatically sorted into course-specific folders (e.g., "History 101", "Calculus II") so you never lose a document.
*   **✅ Smart Download Tracking**: Visual indicators stay active to show you exactly which files you've already grabbed—no more duplicate downloads.
*   **🔍 Power Scenarios**:
    *   **Direct Scans**: Finds files directly on any Canvas page.
    *   **Deep Scraper**: Proactively crawls sub-modules and intermediate pages to find hidden PDFs.
*   **🛡️ Private & Secure**: Runs entirely in your browser. No data ever leaves your computer.

### 🎓 The Ultimate Study Companion...
*   **Semester Archiving**: Save your entire course history before you lose access.
*   **Offline Mastery**: Download all readings for flights, commutes, or coffee shop deep-work sessions.
*   **Data Integrity**: Ensure you have every graded assignment and rubric backed up locally.
